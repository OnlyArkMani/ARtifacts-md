1. Introduction

What this concept is

PyTorch is a tensor library and deep learning framework with dynamic computation graphs, autograd, and extensive model APIs.

Why it exists

To enable research and production ML with intuitive Pythonic interfaces.


2. Key topics

- Tensors, autograd, optimizers, data loaders
- Model definition with nn.Module and training loop
