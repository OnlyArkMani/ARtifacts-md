# 41_NLP

1. Introduction

What this concept is

Natural Language Processing covers tokenization, embeddings, sequence models, transformers, and downstream tasks like classification, QA, generation.

Why it exists

To process and generate human language for applications like search, assistants, summarization.


2. Key ideas

- Tokenization and subword units (BPE)
- Pretrained transformer models and fine-tuning
- Evaluation metrics: BLEU, ROUGE, F1


3. 5-min revision

Use pretrained transformers, be mindful of context length and tokenization effects.
